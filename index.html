What MemeAfterlife records

  1. Public launch details and dated announcements.
  2. Important on-chain events, with transaction links when available.
  3. Community archives and revival proposals.
  4. Sources, uncertainty, and corrections.

A falling price alone does not prove that a token is abandoned or fraudulent. The archive should distinguish verified facts, claims, and opinions.
